ABOUT ME

Hello! I am Anders Moranchek, a freshman here at Indiana University. I was born in Naperville, Illinois, right outside of Chicago, and I have lived there my whole life.

Outside of school, I love spending time with my friends and family. My main hobbies are things like the gym, photography, playing video games, and sports like bowling, track and field.

When it comes to reading and writing, I genuinely enjoy telling stories. Because of my ADHD, reading can sometimes be a bit of a challenge for me, but I have developed some tips and strategies that help me stay focused.
